# Reference Research Behaviors

Use these as behavioral checks, not as a claim that any one project is a complete solution.

## STORM / Co-STORM

Operationalize perspective discovery before outline generation. Simulate distinct expert/stakeholder questions, collect evidence from their conversations, then revise the field outline. Save perspective questions, research notes, and outline revisions. Passing behavior means the final structure contains important angles that were not simply copied from the original prompt.

## GPT Researcher

Operationalize recursive query → learning → gap/follow-up expansion, source curation, and empty-evidence abstention. Passing behavior means cluster research visibly changed its queries after early findings and the report refuses conclusions where evidence remains empty.

## Open Deep Research

Operationalize supervisor → focused workers → raw notes → compressed cluster packets → final synthesis. Passing behavior means important clusters were researched independently, compressed into stable packets, and the executive writer consumed those packets rather than raw search results.

## dzhng/deep-research

Operationalize bounded breadth/depth recursion and follow-up generation, while preserving source-to-claim links that the minimal reference implementation does not. Passing behavior means each cycle records what it learned, what new question followed, and why it stopped.

## Direct comparison

Before delivery, compare the actual artifacts with these behaviors:

- Were multiple perspectives generated and did they change the map?
- Did early findings create new queries or only confirm the initial outline?
- Were important clusters assigned independent deep work units?
- Were raw notes compressed into cluster syntheses before the executive report?
- Did the final writer connect research branches and preserve conflicts?
- Did evidence gaps cause abstention or targeted follow-up?

Do not mark a behavior present because the Skill has a similarly named stage. Point to the exact artifact that demonstrates it.
